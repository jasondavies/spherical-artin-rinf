# Lean formalisation

Lean verifies the [direct Artin-group argument](DIRECT_PROOF.md) for D5, E6, E7, E8, F4, H3 and H4. Appendix C of the paper summarises this route and its dependencies. The general lattice fixed-vector argument is independent of the Artin presentation. Lean also proves Proposition C for the actual presented quotient A(I₂(5))/[P,P], including its identification with the concrete affine extension and the exact Reidemeister number 18. The full quotient classification and its uniform family proofs are **not formalised**.

## Build and check

Lean 4 and Mathlib are pinned by `lean-toolchain`, `lakefile.toml` and `lake-manifest.json`. After installing Elan and obtaining the Mathlib cache:

```sh
cd lean
lake exe cache get
cd ..
python3 scripts/verify.py --only lean --clean
```

The runner cleans only `artin_rinf`, retaining dependency build outputs, then builds the project and runs `scripts/print_axioms.lean`. It validates the axiom lists and writes logs to a separate `verification/runs/<run-id>/` directory. A later component run requires another full run before packaging. Four Lean threads are used by default; change this with `--threads N`. Some certificate modules can require about 25 GB each. A clean build is substantially slower than an incremental one.

The development uses no `sorry`, custom `axiom`, or `native_decide`. Finite data are checked with `decide +kernel`. The reported axioms are only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

## Main results and hypotheses

For each X in D5, E6, E7, E8, F4, H3, H4, the theorem
`ArtinRinf.X.artin_X_R_infty_FH` concludes that every automorphism of the presented Artin group has infinitely many twisted conjugacy classes. Its hypotheses are:

| Hypothesis | Meaning | External input |
|---|---|---|
| `hW` | The Coxeter group is finite. | Classification of finite Coxeter groups. |
| `hCP` | The pure subgroup is characteristic. | Cohen–Paris, Theorem 1.4. |
| `hBr` | The pure abelianisation is abstractly free abelian of rank equal to the number of reflections. | Arrangement-complement homology. |
| `hFH` | Every Coxeter automorphism is a reflection-preserving automorphism composed with a central twist. | Franzsen–Howlett, Theorem 31. |

The equivariant permutation-lattice identification is proved from `hBr`; it is not assumed. None of the four cited inputs is formalised here.

The theorems `H3.artin_H3_R_infty'` and `F4.artin_F4_R_infty'` require only `hW`, `hCP`, `hBr`: their full holonomy automorphism statements are checked by enumeration. The separate theorems `X.artin_X_R_infty` take `hOut`, asserting that the full holonomy automorphism group is generated by inner automorphisms and the specified representative. These are alternative interfaces with different assumptions.

| I₂(5) extension, affine automorphism and exact count | `Dihedral5/*.lean` |

| Presented Artin group, affine map, equivariant pure abelianisation | `ArtinGroup.lean`, `Aff.lean`, `Pure.lean` |
| Orbital certificates and multiplicity-one constituents | `Certificate.lean`, `Types/X.lean` |

The formal constituent certificates use integer bases found computationally, not a formalisation of the paper's graph, frame and icosian constructions. The rank-uniform classical arguments and the hand proof of the E8 holonomy theorem are also outside this development.

The finite reflection tables are linked to the Coxeter group by generator relations and equivariance. A complete abstract identification of their index set with the reflection set is not formalised; the number of reflections appears in `hBr`.

## Proposition C for the presented I₂(5) quotient

`Dihedral5.Gamma` is the infinite subgroup of ℤ⁵ ⋊ D₁₀ with the ten parity cosets specified in `Data.lean`. Its translation kernel is 2ℤ⁵. The development proves:

- `phi` is an automorphism of this extension, with an explicit inverse.
- `affineMap_injective` realizes the extension faithfully as integral affine transformations.
- `generated_eq_top` identifies the extension with the subgroup generated by the two affine Artin images; `braid_relation` checks their five-term Artin relation.
- `reduce` sends every element, by translation-twisted conjugacy, to one of 140 finite states. `encode_twist` proves compatibility with arbitrary twisted conjugation.
- `classification` proves invariance, completeness and representatives for 18 labels. `reidemeister_phi` therefore counts twisted classes of the infinite group, not merely orbits of an auxiliary finite set.
- `exists_reidemeister_eighteen` transfers the result along an explicitly supplied group isomorphism.

`Dihedral5.Presentation` identifies this model with the presented Artin quotient:

- `M := CoxeterMatrix.I 3` has off-diagonal entries 5 (Mathlib's parameter is shifted by two), and `A := ArtinGroup M` is the two-generator group with the five-term braid relation.
- `toGamma` is the surjection taking the two Artin generators to the displayed affine elements.
- `SchreierKernel.closure_edges` proves the general kernel-generation lemma for a chosen section. `Presentation.lean` supplies all ten dihedral coset representatives and reduces their twenty Schreier edges to five meridians using the braid relation.
- `kernel_eq_pure` proves that this ten-coset kernel equals the standard pure subgroup `ArtinGroup.pure M` defined by the Coxeter projection.
- `meridian_image` identifies the five affine images with the standard basis translations. Their generation and these images prove `abelianHalf_bijective`, without an arrangement-homology or rank hypothesis.
- `toGamma_kernel` proves ker(toGamma) = [P,P]. `quotientEquiv` then constructs the isomorphism A(I₂(5))/[P,P] ≃ Gamma.
- `artinQuotient_exists_reidemeister_eighteen` proves that an automorphism of that presented quotient has Reidemeister number 18. `artinQuotient_not_R_infty` states the resulting failure of R∞.

These final theorems have no external mathematical hypotheses and require no supplied group isomorphism. Their axiom reports contain only Lean's standard `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. This formalises Proposition C, including the presentation-to-affine-model step; it does not formalise the full quotient classification.

`LatticeCriterion.lean` similarly separates the common linear mechanism from the Artin-specific reduction. It proves the rational scalar restriction to ±1 and the stable/swap fixed-vector argument, with scalar-action and witness hypotheses stated explicitly. It does not automatically establish these hypotheses for every quotient in Theorem B.

Regenerate the I₂(5) certificates with `python3 lean/scripts/export_dihedral5.py` from the repository root, or pass `--check` to compare without writing. The exporter uses exact SymPy arithmetic and the affine matrices in `code/indep/dihedral_quotient.py`; its output is independently checked by the Lean kernel. The verification runner includes the comparison.

## Certificate data

`Data/*.lean` contains generator permutations, orbital labels, bases, witness words, reflection permutations, and search trees. The kernel checks the braid/Coxeter relations, invariance, scalar orbital actions, sign witnesses, normalising permutations, reflection equivariance and the search trees. An incorrect export causes the corresponding proof to fail.

The search-tree argument uses orbit–stabiliser reductions on tuples of reflection images. Its seven trees have 5–9 nodes and 21–241 admissible extensions. It does not enumerate the full E8 holonomy group.

The untrusted exporters and generators are in `scripts/`: `export_data.py`, `export_out.py`, `export_tree.py`, `gen_types.py`, `gen_fh.py`, and `split_e8.py`. `BT.lean` supplies balanced lookup trees for large data tables.

## The D5 matrix

In the pinned Mathlib release, `CoxeterMatrix.D 5` contains a triangle and is not the D5 matrix. `CoxD5.lean` defines the intended matrix explicitly and proves `mathlib_D5_differs`. The diagram is the chain 0–1–2–3 with node 4 attached to 2. Its certificate uses the ten-dimensional signed-pair constituent U⁻. This is a current dependency issue, not an additional mathematical assumption.
