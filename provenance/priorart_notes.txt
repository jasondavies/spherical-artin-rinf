Prior-art sweep notes: R-infinity for exceptional spherical Artin groups (E6,E7,E8,F4,H3,H4,D5)
Date: 2026-11-07

Logs and raw output: arxiv/log*.txt, arxiv/*.xml, arxiv/html/*.html, other/*.json, cit/*.json, dl/*.txt

KEY FINDINGS
- Soroko arXiv:2610.13904 (9 Oct 2026), section 1, gives a list of Artin types known to have R-inf:
  A_n [FGD10]; P[A_n] [DGO21]; A_n, B_n, D4, I2(m) and their pure subgroups [CS22];
  "D_n, n>=5, in [SV24]"; affine A~, C~ [CS22]; RAAGs; large hyperbolic-type [SV24].
  The list has no E/F/H types and no claim about them. Its open questions (sec. 5) are about
  nonspherical pure subgroups, Aut acylindrical hyperbolicity and twisted Coxeter elements.
- SV24 v2 (Feb 2026) says D5 is still open and states it as a conjecture. Soroko's Oct 2026 paper
  credits D5 to SV24, presumably the updated journal version, using Paris-Soroko Aut(D5).
  Paris-Soroko (2606.26082) never mentions R-inf.
- Soroko 2610 Thm 1: every length-preserving automorphism (this includes global inversion) of a
  NONSPHERICAL Artin group has R = infinity. This largely covers our "bonus" proposition.
- Fel'shtyn-Troitsky arXiv:1204.3175 claims every f.g. residually finite non-amenable group has R-inf.
  That would imply our result, but SV24 sec. 6 reports that Troitsky says the proof contains a
  mistake. Now Conjecture (NA); see Kourovka 19.28. Must be addressed in any manuscript.
- General criteria (FFW23 Thm E; FFIMSW Cor 8.1.4; SV26 Prop 3 / Cor 5-6) need a hyperbolic action
  of Aut(G), or of <Inn(G),phi>, that is non-elementary on Inn. For A/Z of E/F/H types this is
  unknown: Aut is unknown, and FFW23 Question 6.1 and Genevois's question are open.
  JMS (2506.17157) Remark 4.8 and footnote 3 suggest extending the Calvez-Wiest action to Aut(A).
  JMS Corollary H (R-inf) needs a separating vertex in the presentation graph and a centreless group,
  so it excludes spherical types.
- Method precedent: DGO21 (pure braid groups) gets eigenvalue 1 on a characteristic abelian section
  (Gamma2/Gamma3) from representation theory of S_{n+1}, citing Stembridge on eigenvalues of
  reflection-group representations. The Dekimpe-Goncalves-Ocampo school uses crystallographic
  quotients B/[P,P] (Goncalves-Guaschi-Ocampo; Marin for all complex reflection groups) together with
  Dekimpe-Penninckx affine conjugation for R-inf of virtual braid groups.
  No paper found that applies H1(P) = Z[R] or A/[P,P] to R-inf of spherical Artin groups.
- Observation (mine, not claimed in the literature): Fel'shtyn-Luchnikov-Troitsky 2015 (as summarised
  in Troitsky 2210.00591 sec. 1) show that a f.g. residually finite group with R(phi) < infinity has
  |Fix(phi)| finite. Spherical Artin groups are linear (Digne; Cohen-Wales), so R(phi) = infinity
  whenever phi(z) = z for a generator z of the centre. Only automorphisms with phi(z) = z^{-1}
  need a real argument.
